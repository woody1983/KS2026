📊 Archmage's Note — Programme Impact on Base Score
Hālau Hula shows the strongest raw effect on ROOT_IKE base scores (+2.89 pts, p=0.053) — borderline significant, the closest any programme has come to crossing p<0.05. ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi shows a consistent +1.24 pts. PBL shows near-zero effect on ROOT_IKE (+0.08 pts), which is architecturally correct: PBL is designed to drive LEAF indicators (Problem Solving, Collaboration, Innovation), not cultural ROOT indicators.
⚠️ Selection Bias Caveat — OSEMN Interpret
The t-test comparisons above reflect unadjusted group means. Students who self-select into Hawaiian language or Hālau Hula programmes are likely more culturally engaged before enrolment — creating pre-existing baseline differences not controlled for here. The observed score gaps may therefore overstate the causal programme effect. On synthetic data this concern is latent (the causal model controls assignment); it becomes critical when real student data is integrated. Future work: propensity score matching or difference-in-differences analysis when longitudinal real data is available.
