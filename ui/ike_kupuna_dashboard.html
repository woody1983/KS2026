📊 Archmage's Note — Programme Impact on Base Score
Programme participation does not show statistically significant impact on raw base scores alone (p > 0.05 for all three). This is expected: the base score captures a single assessment snapshot. The composite formula is where participation is rewarded — a fully-enrolled student (Hawaiian Language + Hula + PBL) earns +10 bonus pts scaled to 25% of the final score, lifting their composite by up to +25 points above their raw base score.
